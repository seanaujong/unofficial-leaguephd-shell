Unofficial League PhD
=====================

This is an unofficial app. It is not made or endorsed by League PhD (leaguephd.com)
or by Riot Games.

What it does on your computer
-----------------------------
- Finds the running League client and reads its local API password from the
  client's process (or its lockfile), so it can read your champ select.
- Reads champ select only, from the client on this computer (127.0.0.1). It never
  sends commands to the client, never picks or bans for you, and doesn't touch
  game memory or files.
- Shows leaguephd.com in a built-in browser. Links to other sites open in your
  normal browser.
- Stores nothing: the built-in browser keeps no cookies or cache on disk.
- No telemetry, no auto-update, no admin rights needed.

What it sends, and to whom
--------------------------
- Your draft (side, bans, picks and your team's roles, as champion IDs) is sent to
  leaguephd.com so it can suggest picks. No summoner names or account IDs are sent.
- leaguephd.com shows ads and uses analytics (for example from Google), which run
  in the app's window as they would in your browser, under leaguephd.com's own
  policies.

To stop all of this, close the app or uninstall it ("Installed apps" in Windows
Settings). It installs into your user profile only.
